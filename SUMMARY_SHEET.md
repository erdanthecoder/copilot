
# One-Page Summary Sheet — Year 1–2 KidLearn

## Maths

**Year 1:** count to 20, compare numbers, add within 10, recognise 2D shapes.  
**Year 2:** add/subtract within 100, use 2/5/10 times tables, tell time, understand simple money.

## English

**Year 1:** phonics, sight words, capital letters, full stops, simple sentences.  
**Year 2:** adjectives, story order, punctuation, past tense, reading fluency.

## Russian

**Year 1:** alphabet, syllables, greetings, simple words such as мама, дом, кот.  
**Year 2:** short sentences, nouns, reading small stories, translating common words.

## Points

- Correct quiz answers earn stars.
- **30 stars = 1 House point**.
- Teachers can award any number of stars or House points.
- No houses/groups are used — only individual points.

## Classroom Rules

1. Learn kindly.
2. Play safely.
3. No sharing private information.
4. Ask a teacher if something feels wrong.
5. Celebrate progress, not just winning.
