LearnKyrgyz × Quoldek

The first collaboration

Two apps that had never worked on the same thing now do. A lesson here becomes a game on Quoldek in one press — the words your class has just met, asked both ways round, on the board while they are still in the room. Nothing is uploaded and nothing has to be signed up for: the whole quiz travels inside the link.

Eagle Hunt is the limited edition we built for it: one canyon in real 3D, a golden eagle in it for every child in the room, and every right answer is a beat of its wings. The bird at the back rides the wind, so nobody is out of it. It is in the game until the end of December.
